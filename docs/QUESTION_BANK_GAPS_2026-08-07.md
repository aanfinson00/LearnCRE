## 10 candidate phrasings

Draft prompts only — not full `SituationalCase` objects. Each still needs a
scenario, data grid, 3–4 options (one `isBest`), takeaway, and tips per the
spec in `docs/agent-instructions.md` before it can ship. Tags below are the
intended `category` / `assetClass` / `roles`.

1. **"Will absorption clear before your DSCR test?"** — `absorption` ·
   `industrial` · `acquisitions, development`. A last-mile industrial building
   delivers into a submarket with a known absorption pace; ask how many months
   until stabilized occupancy trips a lender's DSCR covenant test date.

2. **"Is 18 months of retail absorption realistic?"** — `absorption` ·
   `retail` · `acquisitions`. A power-center redevelopment needs three
   big-boxes re-tenanted; given a trailing leasing pace and two LOIs in hand,
   ask the learner to sanity-check the sponsor's stabilization timeline.

3. **"Which comps actually price this deal?"** — `comp-selection` ·
   `multifamily` · `acquisitions`. Five multifamily trade comps with mixed
   vintage, renovation scope, and financing type (cash vs. assumable debt);
   ask which to keep, which to discard, and why the assumable-debt comp needs
   an adjustment rather than a discard.

4. **"How do you comp a hotel with no trades nearby?"** — `comp-selection` ·
   `hotel` · `acquisitions, assetManagement`. Thin transaction data forces a
   choice between out-of-market hotel trades and RevPAR-multiple proxies from
   public REIT disclosures; ask which approach the learner would lead with in
   an IC memo.

5. **"Percentage rent: is the breakpoint even in play?"** — `lease-econ` ·
   `retail` · `assetManagement, acquisitions`. A retail tenant's sales/SF and
   base rent are given; ask the learner to compute the natural breakpoint and
   determine whether percentage rent will ever kick in at current sales
   trends.

6. **"NER math on a below-market industrial renewal."** — `lease-econ` ·
   `industrial` · `assetManagement`. A renewal offers free rent plus a TI
   allowance against a below-market face rent; ask which of two competing
   renewal structures nets the landlord more on a net-effective-rent basis.

7. **"What happens to your spread if rates move 100 bps mid-construction?"**
   — `sensitivity` · `development` role. A construction loan is floating-rate
   with an interest-rate cap; ask how a 100 bps move changes the
   development spread and whether the cap fully insulates the sponsor.

8. **"Stress-testing the DSCR covenant on rate reset."** — `sensitivity` ·
   `office` · `mortgageUw`. An office loan is coming up on a rate reset from
   a low fixed rate to a materially higher current rate; ask the learner to
   flag the DSCR headroom (or lack of it) post-reset.

9. **"Are these land comps even comparable?"** — `comp-selection` ·
   `development` role. Raw land trades vary by entitlement status
   (unentitled vs. shovel-ready) and zoning; ask which comps are usable for
   a residual land value analysis and which need a discount for entitlement
   risk.

10. **"How fast does a hotel actually ramp to stabilized RevPAR?"** —
    `absorption` · `hotel` · `assetManagement, acquisitions`. A newly
    opened select-service hotel is 6 months post-open; given a
    ramp-to-stabilization curve assumption and current occupancy/ADR trend,
    ask when the asset likely hits stabilized RevPAR for refinance purposes.
