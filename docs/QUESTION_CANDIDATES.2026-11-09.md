# Candidate question phrasings — 2026-11-09

**Why these areas:** the Supabase `learncre` project is paused, so approval counts could not be read. Coverage was measured from shipped situational cases instead (72 total). Thinnest areas:
- Role `development`: 9 tags (vs. 40 acquisitions)
- Asset class: hotel 1, retail 2, industrial 3 (vs. multifamily 11)
- Category: absorption 1, sensitivity 2 (vs. 7 each for risk/pricing/diagnostic)

These are drafts for review. Not registered in code. Format: scenario → question → best answer / main distractors.

1. **Development · multifamily · absorption — "Lease-up pace vs. pro forma"**
   Scenario: 240-unit deal, pro forma assumes 20 leases/mo; after 4 months you've signed 52 with 6 weeks free rent. Q: What is the best first response? Best: re-run the lease-up on net effective rent and actual pace, then size the extra interest reserve. Distractors: raise asking rents; ignore as seasonal; replace the property manager.
2. **Development · industrial — "Spec vs. build-to-suit"**
   Scenario: 300k SF spec box, submarket vacancy 4%, 2 competing spec projects. Q: Which risk most justifies a higher return hurdle than a BTS? Best: lease-up/exit-cap risk with no signed tenant. Distractors: construction cost, entitlement, tax.
3. **Development · all — "Yield on cost spread"**
   Scenario: Untrended YOC 6.1%, market cap 5.5%, construction costs up 8%. Q: Is the deal still viable? Best: spread is ~60 bps, below the typical 125–150 bps development premium; rebid or restructure.
4. **Development · mortgageUw — "Interest reserve sizing"**
   Scenario: 24-month construction loan, 18-month schedule, 12-month lease-up. Q: What should the interest reserve cover? Best: construction period plus lease-up to stabilization, with a cushion. Distractors: construction only; 12 months flat.
5. **Retail · acquisitions — "Co-tenancy trigger"**
   Scenario: Grocery-anchored center, anchor (40% of GLA) has a go-dark right, and 6 inline tenants have co-tenancy clauses. Q: Biggest underwriting risk? Best: anchor dark → cascading rent reductions/terminations; underwrite the downside.
6. **Retail · assetManagement — "Percentage rent breakpoint"**
   Scenario: Natural breakpoint vs. negotiated artificial breakpoint at renewal. Q: Which favors landlord? Best: natural breakpoint (base rent ÷ % rate).
7. **Industrial · portfolioMgmt — "Mark-to-market concentration"**
   Scenario: 30% of portfolio rent is 40% below market, rolling in 24 months. Q: What does this imply for NOI and valuation? Best: embedded upside but re-leasing/capex risk; stress at 50–75% capture.
8. **Hotel · acquisitions — "RevPAR vs. GOP margin"**
   Scenario: RevPAR +6% but GOP margin −300 bps. Q: What explains it? Best: rate-driven growth was offset by wage/OPEX inflation; check ADR vs. occupancy mix.
9. **Sensitivity · all — "Exit cap vs. rent growth"**
   Scenario: Base IRR 15%; +50 bps exit cap costs 180 bps IRR; −1% rent growth costs 120 bps. Q: Which assumption deserves the most diligence? Best: exit cap (largest sensitivity, least controllable).
10. **Sensitivity · mortgageUw — "DSCR under rate shock"**
    Scenario: Floating loan, DSCR 1.45x at SOFR 4.5%, 1.15x at 6.0%, cap strike 5.5%. Q: What does the cap buy? Best: DSCR floor above 1.2x only up to the strike; size cap tenor to business plan, not loan term.
