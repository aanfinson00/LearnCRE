## Candidates

1. **Hotel · beginner · acquisitions — "Why RevPAR isn't the whole story"**
   A 150-key select-service hotel shows RevPAR up 6% year over year, but GOP is flat. What most likely explains it? (ADR discounting to buy occupancy, rising labor and OTA commissions, versus a data error.)

2. **Hotel · intermediate · mortgageUw — "Sizing debt on a flagged property"**
   A lender is sizing a loan on a hotel whose franchise agreement expires in 18 months. Which underwriting adjustment matters most? (PIP reserve, renewal-risk holdback, versus ignoring it because the franchise is investment grade.)

3. **Retail · beginner · assetManagement — "Reading a percentage-rent clause"**
   An inline tenant pays $30/SF base rent with 6% percentage rent over a $500/SF natural breakpoint. Sales are $560/SF. What is the percentage rent owed per SF? Which input do most learners get wrong?

4. **Retail · intermediate · acquisitions — "Co-tenancy trap"**
   A grocery-anchored center has an inline co-tenancy clause that lets 40% of tenants drop to 50% rent if the anchor goes dark. The anchor lease expires in 24 months with no renewal options. How should the buyer price this?

5. **Industrial · beginner · acquisitions — "Clear height and functionality"**
   Two 100,000 SF warehouses lease at the same rent, but one has 24' clear height and the other 36'. Which is more likely to see faster re-leasing, and why?

6. **Industrial · intermediate · portfolioMgmt — "Mark-to-market concentration"**
   A portfolio's industrial leases roll 35% in the next 18 months at about 30% below market. How should this change the hold-versus-sell view? (Roll risk is an upside source, but only if the market holds.)

7. **Development · beginner · development — "Yield on cost vs. market cap"**
   A ground-up multifamily deal shows a 6.2% yield on cost, and stabilized comps trade at a 5.0% cap. What is the development spread, and is it enough? Include the rule-of-thumb 100–150 bps range.

8. **Development · intermediate · development — "Absorption versus lease-up timing"**
   A 300-unit project delivers in phases of 100 units per quarter. The market absorbs 25 units a month. When does the last phase stabilize at 93%, and what does that do to interest carry?

9. **Development · advanced · development — "Sensitivity on a cost overrun"**
   Hard costs rise 8% mid-construction. Rank which levers (rent premium, exit cap, schedule, scope cut) most efficiently restore a 15% levered IRR, and say what each costs the deal.

10. **Cross-asset · beginner · mortgageUw — "Which NOI goes in the DSCR?"**
    A borrower's T-12 NOI includes a one-time $200K lease termination fee. Which NOI should the underwriter use for the debt-service coverage test, and why?
