These are **unreviewed candidate phrasings**, not registered cases. Numbers are illustrative; verify the math before building any into a `SituationalCase`.

## Absorption (1 existing)
1. A submarket has 6,000 SF/month of net absorption against 240,000 SF of vacant space and 90,000 SF under construction. Roughly how many months of supply does that represent, and what should that change in your lease-up assumption?
2. Your pro forma assumes 20 units/month lease-up. The three nearest comps are doing 8–12/month with concessions. Which assumption do you challenge first, and how do you re-underwrite?
3. Gross absorption is strongly positive but net absorption is flat. What is most likely happening in the submarket, and why does it matter to a buyer?
4. A 300-unit property delivers into a submarket where 2,000 units delivered in the past 18 months. How do you size the concession burn during lease-up?
5. Pre-leasing on a spec industrial building is 0% at 60 days before TCO. What do you do with the stabilization date and the interest reserve?

## Comp selection (2 existing)
6. Three sale comps are 40 bps tighter than the subject's market cap rate, and all three were 1031 buyers. How much weight do they get?
7. The best comp is across the street but sold two years ago before rates moved 150 bps. Do you use it, adjust it, or drop it?
8. A broker's comp set mixes Class A trophy and Class B value-add. How do you rebuild a defensible set for an IC memo?

## Lease economics (2 existing)
9. A tenant has a 3% annual bump and a 5-year term, but the market rent is growing 5%. At what point does the "built-in growth" stop being a positive in your valuation?
10. Two leases show the same $30/SF face rent: one NNN with 2 months free, one modified gross with a $40/SF TI. Which has higher net effective rent over a 10-year term, and what do you need to know first?
