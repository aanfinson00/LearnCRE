# CRE Ethics & Professional Conduct

**Category:** trust-relationships
**Audience:** career-switcher | analyst

## Setup narrative

You are an experienced real estate professional looking to maintain high ethical standards in your transactions and relationships within the industry. You need to ensure that all parties involved, including clients, partners, and vendors, are treated fairly and with integrity. Let's explore some scenarios where these principles come into play.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Fair Dealing
- **Prompt:** Your client has invested $2 million in a new property development project. If you aim to ensure fair dealing by maintaining at least a 3% profit margin for your client, what is the minimum value of the property upon completion if all costs are covered and no additional fees are considered?
- **Expected:** $2,061,224
- **Unit:** usd
- **Hint:** Consider how much revenue must be generated to cover initial investment and desired profit margin.
- **Result description:** Ensuring a fair dealing involves protecting the client's financial interests by setting realistic targets that account for costs and expected profits. This step highlights the importance of transparency in financial dealings.

### Step 2 — Conflict of Interest
- **Prompt:** You are advising on two projects: Project A with an estimated value of $15 million, and Project B worth $3 million. If a potential conflict arises where you could benefit personally by directing funds from both to your own venture, what is the combined total value under advisement to ensure transparency?
- **Expected:** $18,000,000
- **Unit:** usd
- **Hint:** Add up all project values involved in a potential conflict.
- **Result description:** Identifying and disclosing any conflicts of interest is crucial for maintaining trust. This step reinforces the need to be transparent about financial interests and avoid situations where personal benefits could interfere with professional duties.

### Step 3 — Client Communication
- **Prompt:** If you communicate key project milestones every month, ensuring each update takes an average of 2 hours to compile detailed reports on a $1 million investment, how many total hours will you spend communicating effectively over one year?
- **Expected:** 24
- **Unit:** hrs
- **Hint:** Consider the monthly time commitment and multiply by 12 months.
- **Result description:** Effective communication with clients is vital for maintaining trust. Regular updates help manage expectations and provide necessary information to keep projects on track.

### Step 4 — Vendor Selection
- **Prompt:** You need to choose a contractor for your latest project worth $5 million. If you aim to ensure fairness by considering bids from at least three vendors, what is the minimum number of contractors you should consider to maintain ethical standards?
- **Expected:** 3
- **Unit:** multiple
- **Hint:** The requirement specifies a minimum number.
- **Result description:** Selecting vendors through an open and competitive process ensures that clients receive the best possible service at fair prices. This step emphasizes the importance of maintaining integrity in procurement.

### Step 5 — Client Confidentiality
- **Prompt:** You are handling sensitive information for a client who is planning to sell their property next year. If you estimate the property will be worth $2 million, how much would be lost if this confidential information was leaked and resulted in a 10% reduction in sale price due to market speculation?
- **Expected:** $200,000
- **Unit:** usd
- **Hint:** Calculate 10% of the estimated value.
- **Result description:** Protecting client confidentiality is crucial for maintaining trust and avoiding significant financial impacts. This step underscores the importance of secure data handling practices.

## Takeaway

Ethical considerations in CRE transactions are paramount to building and maintaining professional relationships based on trust, transparency, and integrity. By applying these principles consistently, you enhance your reputation as a reliable and trustworthy advisor within the industry.
