# Due Diligence for Commercial Leases

**Category:** legal-document  
**Audience:** analyst | career-switcher  

## Setup narrative

You are tasked with conducting due diligence on a commercial lease before signing it. The property in question is a 10,000 square foot office space that will be leased at $25 per square foot annually for five years. Your goal is to verify the financial viability of this lease and identify any potential risks or opportunities.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Annual Rent Payment
- **Prompt:** Calculate the annual rent payment.
- **Expected:** 250,000 usd
- **Hint:** Remember to multiply the rental rate per square foot by the total area in square feet.
- **Result description:** The calculation of annual rent highlights the direct financial commitment required each year under this lease agreement. Understanding the yearly expense is crucial for budgeting and assessing cash flow implications.

### Step 2 — Total Lease Cost
- **Prompt:** Calculate the total cost over five years, assuming no rental increases.
- **Expected:** 1,250,000 usd
- **Hint:** Multiply your annual rent payment by the length of the lease term to get a sense of overall financial commitment.
- **Result description:** The total cost over five years provides insight into the long-term financial impact of leasing this property. It's essential for evaluating whether the investment aligns with business growth projections and capital allocation strategies.

### Step 3 — Security Deposit
- **Prompt:** Assuming a standard security deposit is equal to two months' rent, calculate the amount.
- **Expected:** 41,667 usd
- **Hint:** Multiply your monthly rental cost by two for this calculation.
- **Result description:** The security deposit serves as collateral against potential damages or unpaid rent. Understanding its value helps in assessing upfront financial requirements and risk exposure.

### Step 4 — Property Taxes
- **Prompt:** If property taxes are estimated to be $1 per square foot annually, calculate the annual tax cost for the tenant.
- **Expected:** 10,000 usd
- **Hint:** Multiply the total area in square feet by the rate per square foot.
- **Result description:** Property taxes can significantly impact operating costs. Knowing this expense helps forecast total occupancy expenses and informs lease negotiation strategies.

### Step 5 — Total Operating Costs
- **Prompt:** Calculate the total annual operating cost including rent, property tax, and assuming utilities are $1,000 per month.
- **Expected:** 271,667 usd
- **Hint:** Add your annual rental payment, property taxes, and utilities for a comprehensive view of yearly expenses.
- **Result description:** Total operating costs provide a clear picture of the financial burden associated with leasing this space. This figure is crucial in evaluating both immediate budget impacts and long-term sustainability.

## Takeaway

By conducting thorough due diligence on commercial lease terms, you can better understand all aspects of your financial commitment, including rent, taxes, and other operational costs. A comprehensive assessment helps protect tenant rights while ensuring business objectives are aligned with the fiscal realities of the lease agreement.
