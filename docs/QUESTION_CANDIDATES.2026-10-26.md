## Situational · absorption
1. A submarket has 6,000 units at 88% occupancy, and 450 new units deliver next quarter. Net absorption has run 60 units/month. How many months until the submarket hits 94%, and how sensitive is that answer to absorption slowing to 45/month?
2. Two competing lease-ups of 250 units each open within 90 days of your property. Combined submarket absorption is 80 units/month. Is your underwritten 20 units/month lease-up pace defensible, and what would you change?

## Situational · comp-selection
3. You have five sale comps: two are 2 years old at 5.5% caps, one is a portfolio sale at 6.0%, and two are recent one-off trades at 6.4%. Which do you weight most for a single-asset pricing opinion, and why?
4. A broker's comp set includes three trades from a different submarket with stronger demographics. When is it reasonable to keep them, and what adjustment would you apply?

## Situational · lease-econ
5. A tenant offers $34/SF gross with 4 months free versus $32/SF gross with 1 month free on a 5-year term. Which has the higher net effective rent, and what does the answer ignore?
6. A landlord can give $60/SF TI on a 7-year lease or $40/SF TI on a 5-year lease at the same face rent. How do you compare them on a per-year-of-term basis?

## Situational · sensitivity
7. Your base-case levered IRR is 14%. Exit cap +50 bps drops it to 11%; rent growth -1% drops it to 12.5%. Which assumption does the deal depend on most, and how do you present that to IC?
8. A 65% LTV refi pencils at 1.35x DSCR at today's rate. At what rate increase does it breach a 1.25x covenant, and which input is most worth stress-testing first?

## Mock interview · fit / marketView
9. (fit) Walk me through why you want to move from a lender-side role to an acquisitions seat, and what you'd miss about underwriting for credit.
10. (marketView) If cap rates are flat but rate-cut expectations shift, which property types do you expect to re-price first, and why?
